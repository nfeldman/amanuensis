# Panel method and lineage accounting

## Objective

The panel was convened to make Amanuensis HTML output unusually delightful, clear, actionable, and orienting without weakening its evidence contract or changing production Amanuensis/AxiomDB files.

## Blind protocol

All panelists received the same sealed brief at `../blind-brief.md` plus the generated AxiomDB report rooted at `/Users/nfeldman/repos/axiomdb/.amanuensis/docs/index.html`. They were forbidden to inspect conversation history, prior critique, prior research, preferred directions, or one another's work. Each worked in a fresh agent context and wrote a memo to disk before any memo was opened by the lead.

The lead waited for an exact 3/3 fan-in. All three memos were then opened together. No panelist deliberation or imitation round occurred.

## Heterogeneity accounting

The disciplines genuinely compete, but the model lineage does not. `gpt-5.6-sol` and `gpt-5.6-terra` are both Codex/OpenAI variants. Their clean contexts support independent readings, not independent model-lineage evidence. No vote count or claim of cross-lineage convergence is made.

An on-device Qwen run was initiated to add a heterogeneous lineage, produced no memo, and was stopped and excluded at the user's request. It is not part of the panel or its evidence. An external Claude route was not used because sending the private report to an external service required specific authorization. The final panel is therefore explicitly Codex-only.

## Moving-source caveat

The generated AxiomDB projection changed while blind readings were underway. This is itself a concrete reorientation problem: stable report paths were regenerated without a reader-facing delta. The blind brief recorded the first observed snapshot (`19 / 34` mapped, `11` confirmed findings); panelists independently encountered later states, including `21 / 34` and `13` findings. At synthesis start, the current projection reported `22 / 34` mapped and `13` confirmed findings at last-checked SHA `d19ade703e3c`.

The synthesis-start state (`22 / 34`, `13` findings) was intermediate. The prototype freezes the later internally coherent 18:15 EDT capture (`25 / 34`, 14 findings: 1 critical and 1 high) and labels it as a representative publication, not a live dashboard. Exact source hashes are in `../source-capture.md`; report claims remain attached to their cited checked SHA.

## Interpretation rule

- Independent agreement among memos is a useful design signal, not empirical validation.
- Research claims are kept in the Scholiast custody workspace and labeled separately from creative judgments.
- The lead may combine, reject, or tension panel proposals, but must preserve the rejected tradeoffs and unresolved disagreements in the synthesis.
