# Product ruling · 2026-08-23

The information-architecture work and the interface-design work are distinct
surveys. Their synthesis must not make one visual concept appear to validate
the other.

## Retained from the IA work

- Keep source alignment, survey coverage, open engineering work, and
  publication integrity as separate dimensions.
- Put consequential findings before exhaustive subsystem inventory.
- Preserve stable routes into subsystem context and supporting evidence.
- Offer report-owned return paths without implying personal history or a
  predicted next task.

## Rejected from the UI direction

- The first viewport tried to present too many concepts at once.
- Beige/parchment color, literal paper affect, and fieldbook costume are
  retired. The original green-gray palette, square geometry, restrained
  outlines, and mid-century typography remain useful character.
- Clinical labels are retired. Use direct software-practitioner language.
- Textual-critical vocabulary is welcome where it precisely names evidence
  relationships, not as visual or verbal costume.

## Record presentation

The renderer must not turn the storage schema into the interface. A focused
Scholiast survey, measured against the current AxiomDB corpus, establishes a
typed presentation grammar:

- keep genuine matrices and lifecycle comparisons as semantic tables;
- render prose-heavy rows as ruled, addressable records;
- group compact metadata as named facts, subordinate to the account a reader
  came to understand;
- read finding behavior and cause in sequence rather than in parallel columns;
- keep paths, revisions, and identifiers visible without giving each a peer
  column; and
- choose by known record type, never by a generic card style or a text-length
  heuristic.

The evidence, corpus profile, and exact schema decisions live in
[`../../scholiast/report-record-presentation/conspectus.md`](../../scholiast/report-record-presentation/conspectus.md).

## Register

Keep `Decision docket` for a bounded set of unresolved decisions and
`Warrants` for the reasoning that connects evidence to a claim. Make the
other swaps:

| Previous label | Current label |
|---|---|
| Casefiles | Findings |
| Four conditions | Project status / status dimensions |
| Resume this report | Continue reviewing |
| Evidence trail | Supporting evidence |
| Unseen territory | Not yet surveyed |

The project name is the primary identity. Amanuensis appears as the producing
method. The revised `prototype-current/index.html` applies these rulings; the
original panel memos and historical prototype remain unchanged as survey
records.
