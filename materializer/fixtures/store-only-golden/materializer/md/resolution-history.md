# Resolution history

*The append-only account of how records reached their state. Use it when you want to know how a record reached its state.*

Terms on this page: [Session](how-to-read.md#term-session)

No resolution event is recorded.

- **Scope** — every state a finding or a contradiction was recorded in, newest first, with the account given at the time.
- **Basis** — `finding_resolution_events` and `contradiction_resolution_events`, the two tables nothing updates or deletes.
- **Checked revision** — `deadbeefdead` on `main`.

*Rendered from the command line. Its read-back was not recorded in the survey's record, so the Publication integrity figures on the overview describe an earlier publication.*
