Scribe

Overview

Use it when you want to know what the codebase is and what its parts are.

Terms on this page

Survey
The agent's reading of the repository, done in passes, with each conclusion tied to the code it read.
Subsystem
A part of the codebase the survey treats as one unit, with its own files and its own page. The agent chose the split during onboarding.
Lens
One of four groups of pages: Codebase (what the project is), Unresolved (open work), History (what was settled), Method (how far to trust the rest).
Thesis
The paragraph on the overview that says what the codebase does.

Reader's guide

Overview

§What Scribe is

Scribe records what a survey of a codebase read, in a database, and publishes pages from it.

This thesis was imported from the entry point, not tied to evidence; a recorded purpose replaces it.

§Its parts

  • Survey method tells the agent what to read and what to record.
  • Record store holds every record the survey writes.
  • Page materializer
