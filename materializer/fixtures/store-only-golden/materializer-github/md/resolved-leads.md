# Resolved leads and questions

*Questions that were answered or dismissed, and leads that were closed. Use it when you want to know how a question was answered.*

Terms on this page: [Lead](how-to-read.md#term-lead) · [Decision needed](how-to-read.md#term-decision-needed)

No question has been settled and no lead has been closed.

- **Scope** — questions answered, dismissed, or superseded, and leads closed either by becoming a finding or by being dismissed.
- **Basis** — `open_questions.resolution` and `field_notes.follow_up`, both mutable columns with no event table behind them.
- **Checked revision** — `deadbeefdead` on `main`.
