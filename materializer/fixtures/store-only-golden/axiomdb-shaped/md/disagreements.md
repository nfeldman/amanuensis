# Records that disagree

*Where credible records disagree or the evidence does not discriminate between them. Use it when you want to know where the record contradicts itself.*

Terms on this page: [Subsystem](how-to-read.md#term-subsystem) · [Evidence](how-to-read.md#term-evidence) · [Claim](how-to-read.md#term-claim) · [Concern review](how-to-read.md#term-concern-review) · [Finding](how-to-read.md#term-finding) · [Disagreement](how-to-read.md#term-disagreement) · [Competing explanations](how-to-read.md#term-competing-explanations)

No record stands undiscriminated.

- **Scope** — findings that cannot both be right, evidence matrices the evidence does not separate, and concern reviews that recorded a competition rather than a verdict.
- **Basis** — `contradictions`, `diagnosticity_sessions`, and `dispositions`, each read for the state that means *the record does not say which*.
