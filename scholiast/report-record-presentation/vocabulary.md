# Vocabulary

- **table** — here, a semantic two-dimensional grid whose row/column
  relationships are part of the information, not any rectangular arrangement
  made with CSS.
- **data list** — a sequence of same-kind records whose internal layout may be
  multiline and responsive. It preserves record grouping without asserting that
  every datum is a comparable column.
- **definition list** — key/value facts about one object, represented with
  `<dl>`, `<dt>`, and `<dd>`.
- **record** — one finding, file-scope entry, disposition, subsystem, symbol, or
  other unit a reader may identify and understand as a whole.
- **card** — a visually bounded record container. A data list item need not be a
  card; cards add stronger separation and usually more whitespace.
- **index/detail** — a compact register for choosing a record plus a separate
  full reading surface for that record.
