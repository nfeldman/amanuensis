Terms on this page

Survey
The agent's reading of the repository, done in passes, with each conclusion tied to the code it read.
Subsystem
A part of the codebase the survey treats as one unit, with its own files and its own page. The agent chose the split during onboarding.
Layer
A group of subsystems with a similar role. Pages group subsystems by layer.
Survey depth
How far the survey of a subsystem has got. Unmapped: not started. Scoped and Structural: that pass is done. Reviewing concerns and Challenging claims: that pass is under way. Mapped: every pass is done. Deferred: left out of the survey on purpose. Each depth limits what the record may claim.
Mapped
The last survey depth: every pass has run. It does not mean every file is read or every bug found.
Scope
The files a subsystem owns, each marked as read, not yet read, or left out with a reason.
Evidence
A pointer to code at a commit (file, symbol, lines) with a note of how strong it is: read in code, seen in a test, stated in a comment, and so on.
Claim
A statement about how the code is built ("this cache is per request"), with evidence.
Challenge
A pass in which the agent tries to prove its own claims and findings wrong. Each claim records whether it survived.
Concern
One kind of defect worth looking for in this codebase, such as a race on a shared cache. The review checklist is the full list.
Concern review
The verdict for one concern in one subsystem: bug found, acceptable, ruled out, out of scope, or undecided.
Finding
A defect the survey confirmed, with evidence, a severity, and a state (open, repaired, verified, ruled out, accepted).
Lead
Something the agent noticed that is not a finding: a pattern, an oddity, a tension, a possible concern.
Decision needed
Something the agent could not settle alone. It records the assumption it used and keeps going; you answer it.
System boundary
A place where two subsystems share something (a table, a queue, a cache) and depend on each other's assumptions about it.
Recorded edge
A dependency or data flow between two subsystems that the agent wrote down. Edges are never guessed from names.
Standing
What the record says about one file: no record, left out, in scope but unread, read, read but changed since.
Purpose, account
A subsystem's one-sentence purpose and its longer explanation of how it works, each written from evidence.

Reader's guide

Research, crosswalk and learning ledger

§Purpose

Outside research and past runs feed later Amanuensis runs, each through a check. A research question goes to Scholiast only if it could move a named decision, and its results stay apart from code claims. A method or lesson from a finished session becomes policy only after a scored test. The crosswalk keeps concepts from different sources distinct until someone resolves them as the same.

Recorded at 94d98773.

§Scope

Decision-bounded research requests, the identity-first crosswalk with qualified methods, and the typed learning ledger and policy; their tests, schema and fixtures.

Start here mcp-server/src/tools/research.ts, then mcp-server/src/tools/crosswalk.ts

§Survey note

11 files, about 5,700 lines.

§How it works

In all three tool modules, a status change commits in one transaction with the rows that record it; a check that fails inside the transaction rolls them back. Triggers in schema.sql enforce the transitions, and some rules live only there: activating an unqualified method or attaching crosswalk data to an unresolved entity fails as a raw SQLite abort, not a ToolError.

research.ts brokers external research for a decision. propose_research_request admits a request only when its expected information value (decision sensitivity times uncertainty reducibility) reaches 9, the local search is exhausted, and the cited evidence quotes its source exactly. Otherwise it defers the request with reasons, or rejects it for a bad destination or a repeated question. An admitted request is dispatched to a scholiast/<slug> directory and lands with its sources and external claims; a claim's confidence is capped by how directly its sources were read.

crosswalk.ts stages named concepts as crosswalk entities. An entity whose normalized label is unique is marked distinct at once; one whose label collides stays pending until resolve_crosswalk_identity settles it against one candidate. The module also qualifies methods. Scoring passes only when the landed result meets the plan's frozen prediction, controls, red gates and custody counts, and the artifact file still has the hash recorded for it. Only a passed qualification can be activated as unattended policy.

learning.ts turns the outcomes of finished agent, review and design sessions into lessons, and one evaluation qualifies a lesson. A staged lesson must name its key's active lesson, if there is one, as its predecessor; verify_learning_policy then supersedes that lesson and activates the new one in one transaction. A method lesson stages only after its crosswalk qualification is activated.

The modules read decision and design-session rows (W-03 (Decisions, design sessions and codebase brief)), claims and evidence (R-03 (Evidence, claims and findings)) and review sessions (W-02 (Review sessions, review analysis and composition)), and write none of them.

§Structure

W-04 keeps three ledgers in SQLite, one per MCP tool module: research.ts, crosswalk.ts and learning.ts. Rows are appended, and a status change is written in one transaction with the row that records why. Handlers check preconditions before writing; triggers re-check only some, not admission's criteria. Some gates live only in triggers: activating a method, qualifying a method lesson, and the crosswalk's identity checks on properties and relations.

A research request is a question. Only a request bound to a field of a decision revision, or to any review hypothesis, can be admitted. propose_research_request scores it, records it as admitted, deferred or rejected, and stores its contract with a hash. An admitted request is handed off to a scholiast/<slug> workspace. Landing hashes the named artifacts, which must lie inside that workspace, and records sources, external claims graded by how directly their sources were read, and contradictions with current code observations. Consuming records the effect on the destination, or why there is none.

The crosswalk stages entities from an existing record or from the user. A new entity whose label collides with a resolved entity's label stays pending until it is resolved against one candidate. Only resolved entities take properties and relations. Method qualification starts from a frozen plan: a prediction, controls, red gates and a custody manifest. One JSON artifact is landed and checked against the reported result, and the result is scored. Only a passed qualification can enter unattended_method_policy.

A lesson comes from the extracted outcome of a terminal session and belongs to one of five channels. It is qualified by one evaluation, staged as a policy version, and activated with read-backs. A method lesson qualifies only against a passed method qualification, and is staged only once that method is active.

The modules read claims, evidence, decision revisions, and review and design sessions by direct SQL, and write none of them.

§Vocabulary

  • crosswalk entity — A named concept from some source (claim, research claim, concern, decision, evidence or direct user) staged with a definition and negative criteria, whose identity is resolved as distinct or same-as before properties and relations may attach.
  • expected information value — The admission score of a research request: decision sensitivity times uncertainty reducibility, which must reach 9 for the request to be admitted.
  • learning lesson — A typed proposition extracted from a terminal session outcome that, once qualified by an evaluation and verified by read-back, becomes the active policy for one policy key.

§Known defects here

4 defects here are open or awaiting verification. Each one's full record, with its evidence, is on Open findings.

  • verify_learning_policy records a preactivation read-back that can never fail. It compares runtimePolicy(row) with runtimePolicy(row) on the same in-memory row, so its refusal is unreachable, and the recorded read-back row is green by construction. — W04-1 · Medium · Open
  • Two W-04 queues have dead ends. - A learning lesson whose one qualification fails stays a candidate permanently and cannot be evaluated again. - A deferred research request can never be admitted: no tool moves deferred to admitted, though the schema allows it. Duplicate detection also counts the deferred request, so an improved resubmission is rejected unless it names a changed premise. — W04-2 · Low · Open
  • W-04 treats records as live without applying their owners' currency rules. A research request may target a rejected, superseded or invalidated decision revision. A crosswalk entity may cite a closed claim as its source. get_learning_ledger lists an active policy that get_learning_policy hides because it has no post-activation read-back. — W04-3 · Low · Open
  • Crosswalk counterevidence can never be resolved, so get_crosswalk's unresolved_contradictions count only grows. The count also includes counterevidence on superseded relations, and the schema's scope-distinction and relation-superseded resolutions are unreachable. — W04-4 · Low · Open
