Rationale

All three sites carried finding B07-1 names are unbounded at HEAD: an awaited fetch with no AbortSignal, a spawnSync of the shadcn CLI with no timeout, and a subprocess.check_output of a child detector with none. Filed as B07-R1 at LOW, because these are operator-run capture scripts rather than a hosted path, so an unresponsive endpoint costs a hung terminal and a re-run.

§Observed
