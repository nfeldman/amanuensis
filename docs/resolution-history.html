Resolution history

Every state a finding or a contradiction was recorded in, and every re-grade of a finding's severity, newest first, with the account given at the time. Nothing here is rewritten when a record moves on: a repair that was later defeated keeps the event that recorded it, which is how a regression is visible at all. Each row links to where that record is written in full today.
