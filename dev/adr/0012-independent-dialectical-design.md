# ADR-0012: Preserve independent design tension and stop at underdetermination

- Status: accepted for A11
- Date: 2026-08-12
- Deciders: Amanuensis roadmap implementation; architecture acceptance remains human-owned

## Context

A design session can become theater: three named personas consume the same pack,
see one another's conclusions, and converge into polished consensus. It can also
overstep authority by interpreting inconsistent desires as permission to choose.
A11 needs useful synthesis without manufacturing either independence or intent.

## Decision

Every session freezes human-origin desires and exactly three lenses:

- **immanent** recovers the existing trajectory without calling accidents intent;
- **adversarial** tries to overturn attractive options against constraints; and
- **speculative** explores adjacent potential while naming foreclosure.

The lenses consume review, design, and generative projections of one immutable
CodebaseBrief source through distinct context profiles. The panel uses at least
two model families and one unlike the orchestrator. All three packets dispatch
before any output lands; packets contain no prior lens output and there is no
deliberation round.

Each lens furnishes at least two options using a fixed matrix: preserves,
rejects, enables, forecloses, migration cost, reversibility, visible evidence,
evidence gaps, falsifiers, and research needs. Evidence citations must have
been visible in that lane's packet. Landing forbids decision/acceptance fields.

Aggregation is mechanical after exact three-lens fan-in. It retains preference
and field-level disagreement. A lean requires a strict independent majority,
decisive evidence, and no unresolved mutually exclusive or highest-priority
desire. The lean is explicitly advice-only and unaccepted. Otherwise the result
is `underdetermined` and names the missing desire or discriminating constraint.

Evaluation packets cover clean, marker-only, treated, and incoherent/null
artifacts. They remove condition, session, provider, model, and lens identity,
anonymize lane contributions, and scan surviving content for caller-supplied
canaries before delivery.

## Consequences and limits

- Meaningful disagreement becomes durable input to choice rather than a defect
  to smooth away.
- Mutually exclusive goals cannot yield a confident recommendation without a
  human priority rule.
- The substrate proves independence and custody of packets, not that three
  model outputs are cognitively independent or equally insightful.
- The fixture proves sensitivity to constraint removal and null rejection; it
  does not estimate real-world quality lift or a model-family premium.
- A12 remains the only path by which furnished advice can become an accepted
  decision.

## Verification obligations

- [x] No lens output lands until all three controlled packets dispatch.
- [x] A majority lean preserves at least one meaningful independent preference
  disagreement and remains advice-only.
- [x] Removing stated constraints lowers measured preservation; restoring them
  raises it.
- [x] Mutually exclusive unprioritized desires produce underdetermination and a
  named missing desire rather than a lean.
- [x] Clean, marker-only, treated, and null packets omit structural identities,
  anonymize lane content, and pass content canaries.
- [x] A landing cannot smuggle decision acceptance fields.
