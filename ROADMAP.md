# Amanuensis roadmap: from surveyed codebase to living conspectus

> Generated from `dev/roadmap.json` by `dev/render-roadmap.mjs`. Do not edit this file directly. Roadmap v1.0, updated 2026-08-12.

## Decision

The ambition is coherent as a program and too broad as a single release. Amanuensis will become the codebase epistemic engine—not a universal orchestration platform, autonomous product manager, or second source of truth for code. The first product proof is a living record that becomes stale precisely, repairs itself selectively, and reduces human verification work. Review, architecture, research learning, and Chorusmith extraction earn their place only after that proof.

**North star:** Once a repository has been fully surveyed, Amanuensis maintains a revision-aware conspectus and can furnish any consequential review, design decision, or generative exploration with a bounded situation model: current facts, traced intent, applicable constraints, known contradictions, invalidated knowledge, plausible options, and explicit unknowns.

## Epistemic baseline

### Observed

- The current product already separates scope, structural, concern, adversarial, and mapped phases, and server-side invariants limit what may be claimed at each depth. Evidence: `README.md`, `mcp-server/src/invariants.ts`.
- The SQLite record already stores evidence, dispositions, findings, contradictions, cross-references, file ownership, query history, and git baselines. Evidence: `mcp-server/src/schema.sql`, `mcp-server/DEVELOPMENT.md`.
- Change detection currently maps changed paths to file-ledger rows and marks every entry in directly affected subsystems stale; it does not compute transitive claim or seam impact. Evidence: `mcp-server/src/tools/git.ts`, `mcp-server/src/tools/stale.ts`.
- Finding repairs now separate fixed-pending-verification from verified-fixed, and both verification and contradiction resolution require structured evidence; append-only events preserve later reopenings and supersession history. Evidence: `mcp-server/src/tools/findings.ts`, `mcp-server/src/invariants.ts`.
- Access and query logs support usage-aware compression, but that mechanism optimizes retrieval rather than learning which claims remain true or which methods work. Evidence: `mcp-server/src/tools/logging.ts`, `mcp-server/src/schema.sql`.
- The repository has broad mechanical validation for tools, schemas, storage, invariants, adversarial behavior, performance, and materialization. Evidence: `.github/workflows/test.yml`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

### Inferred, not yet established

- **Inference:** Temporal validity and targeted revalidation are the highest-leverage missing substrate. **Basis:** Review, architecture, and research learning all become unsafe if a changed file can leave plausible but invalid claims looking current. **Counterpoint:** A coarser subsystem-staleness model may prove sufficient for smaller repositories; the roadmap therefore requires comparative cost and recall measurements before deeper graph machinery is accepted.
- **Inference:** Amanuensis can support architecture work without becoming the owner of goals or decisions. **Basis:** Its evidence, constraints, contradictions, and history can compile the situation in which a decision is made. **Counterpoint:** Inferring intent from code can fossilize accidents; direct intent, inferred intent, and observed behavior must remain separate kinds.
- **Inference:** Crosswalk-driven research can improve decisions only if questions are admitted and later reconciled against their decision impact. **Basis:** Unbounded topic generation creates an attractive research backlog with no obligation to change a design or close a gap. **Counterpoint:** Some exploratory research has delayed value; the system must retain bounded speculative questions without pretending they are validated product work.

## Product boundaries

### Tensions we intend to preserve

| Tension | First pole | Second pole | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Record versus reality | A durable record prevents repeated work. | The repository and its behavior remain the implementation truth. | Amanuensis records claims about code with validity, provenance, and invalidation; it never treats its prose as more authoritative than the referenced code state. |
| Continuity versus staleness | Carry knowledge across sessions. | Changed code can make yesterday's knowledge dangerous. | Preserve historical claims while removing current authority until targeted revalidation succeeds. |
| Automation versus desire | Run the epistemic loop unattended end to end. | A model must not invent product goals or silently accept architecture decisions. | Automate observation, challenge, maintenance, option generation, and verification; reserve desire, irreversible policy, and decision acceptance for humans. |
| Density versus proof | Give reviewers and designers a high-value brief. | Compression can erase uncertainty and provenance. | Compile task-specific briefs from typed claims, with expandable evidence and explicit omitted/unknown sections. |
| Product identity versus platform duplication | Amanuensis should feel like a complete code intelligence product. | Chorusmith is the natural home for general session orchestration and context compilation. | Keep Amanuensis complete as a domain engine and user-facing workflow; expose stable contracts so Chorusmith can orchestrate it later without an early rewrite. |
| Immanent design versus speculative potential | Honor what the system is already trying to do. | Do not let current accidents cap its potential. | Generate three labeled lenses—immanent, adversarial, and speculative—without merging them into a false consensus. |

### Operational definitions

#### Fully surveyed

At a named commit: every in-scope file is assigned or explicitly excluded; every subsystem has completed the required phase sequence; every declared seam has both endpoints mapped and an integral assessment; every applicable concern has an evidence-backed disposition or a tracked gap; dispatched, landed, and scored work reconcile; and the clean export passes read-back validation.

Does not mean:

- It does not mean the repository contains no defects.
- It does not mean every open question is answered.
- It does not mean a green collection of subsystem checks proves the integrated HEAD state.

#### System of record

The authoritative ledger of what Amanuensis has established, rejected, invalidated, or left open about a codebase, including the evidence and repository state that bound each claim.

Does not mean:

- It is not the source of truth for code behavior; code and runtime evidence remain primary.
- It is not a wiki whose uncited prose becomes authoritative through repetition.
- It is not a substitute for accepted product intent or architecture decisions.

#### Living conspectus

A system of record that detects relevant repository changes, predicts affected knowledge, withdraws stale authority, schedules bounded revalidation, records supersession, and verifies that regenerated projections match durable state.

Does not mean:

- It is not a periodic full rescan with a new timestamp.
- It is not query-frequency compression mislabeled as learning.
- It is not automatic rewriting of claims without retained history.

#### Super review

A change review that combines the diff with the pre-existing conspectus, predicted blast radius, historical findings, cross-seam obligations, invalidated claims, adversarial challenge, and an integral check of the assembled HEAD state.

Does not mean:

- It is not a larger list of plausible defects.
- It is not a unit-test summary.
- It is not a claim of safety when known gaps or unlanded work remain.

#### Architecture session

A bounded decision-support process that compiles observed behavior, direct and inferred intent, constraints, contradictions, options, consequences, falsifiers, and unresolved research into a reviewable decision record.

Does not mean:

- It does not let the model originate the user's desire.
- It does not collapse evidence, interpretation, and recommendation into one voice.
- It does not accept or execute an architecture decision on the user's behalf.

#### Learning

A traceable change to corpus state, retrieval policy, method choice, research backlog, or user preference that follows from observed outcomes and can itself be revised.

Does not mean:

- It is not merely storing more text.
- It is not tuning behavior from an unscored conversation.
- It is not importing external research without provenance or decision relevance.

### Automation and authority

Amanuensis may run unattended to:

- Read configured repository and conspectus sources, detect changes, and update derived indexes.
- Invalidate current authority, create obligations, schedule revalidation, and retain historical claims.
- Generate review briefs, architecture options, adversarial challenges, research questions, and draft decision records.
- Run configured tests, controls, bounded relational checks, materialization, and read-back verification.
- Use already configured analysis and research providers within explicit budget, source, and data boundaries.

It must stop or produce a proposal when:

- A new product desire, priority, or risk appetite is required.
- A data source, API, repository, spend boundary, or external audience is outside the configured envelope.
- An architecture decision, policy exception, code merge, deployment, or destructive action would be accepted or executed.
- Two valid options remain and the choice depends on preference rather than evidence.

Hard stops enforced by the system:

- No finding, fixed status, or current claim may cross its evidence gate.
- No blocking obligation may be hidden by a green aggregate status.
- No run may report complete when dispatched, landed, and scored sets differ.
- No generated projection may publish when state, coverage, or content read-back disagrees with durable storage.

## Operating principles

- **P1 — Temporal authority, permanent history:** Claims can lose present authority without being deleted; supersession and invalidation are evidence-bearing events. _Catalog: GP11, GP18, GP23._
- **P2 — Observations before interpretations:** Every brief and record keeps observed facts, inferences, hypotheses, questions, desires, and decisions distinguishable. _Catalog: GP1, GP12, GP16._
- **P3 — Generators do not certify themselves:** A separate verifier checks findings, completion, projections, and high-impact recommendations; programmatic gates halt progress when feasible. _Catalog: GP13, GP19, GP25, VP4._
- **P4 — Composition is its own proof obligation:** Subsystem success never substitutes for fan-in reconciliation, seam review, or an integral HEAD lane. _Catalog: GP21, GP22, GP24._
- **P5 — Unknowns have destinations:** A gap must create a visible obligation owned by a later pass, a research queue, or a human decision; otherwise completion remains red. _Catalog: GP9, GP20, GP23._
- **P6 — Start with explicit relations:** Use registries, lexical structure, git history, and bounded relational cores before models, embeddings, ontologies, or graph infrastructure. _Catalog: GP27, GP35._
- **P7 — Measure the reduction in verification burden:** Success is not output volume; it is more correct action with less repeated human checking, without suppressing uncertainty. _Catalog: GP29, GP31, GP32, VP6._

## Roadmap

### Now — make the record alive

**Horizon:** First 6–10 engineering weeks; complete before expanding the product surface.

**Objective:** Prove that a completed survey can stay trustworthy across repository changes with less work than resurveying it.

**Stage exits:**

- The full-survey definition is computed and cannot go green with missing files, seams, dispositions, runs, or read-back mismatches.
- Known claim-invalidating changes are detected and revalidated with target recall while benign changes stay below the false-invalidation guardrail.
- A fixed finding cannot become verified-fixed without new evidence at the repaired revision.
- An unattended refresh completes within configured authority and stops visibly at every hard gate.
- The living-record path beats full resurvey on human verification minutes without worsening known-defect recall.

#### A0 — Dogfood baseline and executable definitions

**Status:** done<br>
**Owner:** Amanuensis<br>
**Depends on:** none<br>
**Metrics:** M1, M11, M12

**Outcome:** Amanuensis can survey itself at a pinned commit, calculate whether that survey is complete, and replay a graded fixture ladder against an immutable baseline.

**Why:** The program needs a falsifiable baseline before schema work. Otherwise every later improvement can be declared successful by changing the meaning of complete.

Deliverables:

- An ADR freezing operational meanings for fully surveyed, current, stale, invalid, resolved, verified-fixed, and complete.
- A self-conspectus fixture pinned to a repository commit with inventory, seam, concern, and run reconciliation manifests.
- A control harness containing null, benign, invalidating, cross-seam, incoherent-request, export, and repeated-run cases.
- A baseline report with denominators, exclusions, runtime configuration, replicate IDs, and minimum detectable effects.

Acceptance:

- The completion checker reports the named missing obligation for each independently removed file assignment, concern disposition, seam endpoint, landed result, and scored result.
- Every gate is demonstrated red at least once before the green baseline is accepted.
- Two unchanged-baseline runs are reported separately and establish metric step size; they are not pooled.
- The baseline export is regenerated in a clean directory and read back against durable counts and identifiers.

**Red gate:** Delete or corrupt one obligation in each control class; if completion stays green or the error does not name the missing destination, A0 fails.

Risks:

- A self-survey can overfit the fixture to Amanuensis's own architecture.
- A single model/runtime baseline can be mistaken for a general reliability estimate.

#### A1 — Temporal claim and supersession model

**Status:** done<br>
**Owner:** Amanuensis<br>
**Depends on:** A0<br>
**Metrics:** M1, M2, M3

**Outcome:** Every authoritative statement is typed, provenance-bearing, and explicitly valid for a repository state; invalidation and supersession preserve history.

**Why:** A living record cannot be built by attaching a stale bit to whole subsystem prose. Review and design both require knowing exactly which claims lost authority and why.

Deliverables:

- A schema ADR comparing commit intervals, supersession edges, and a combined model against real query patterns.
- Additive migrations for claims, claim-evidence links, validity events, and epistemic kinds.
- Write-path invariants that reject untyped authority, missing provenance, invalid commit intervals, and unsupported supersession.
- A compatibility projection that maps existing entries, evidence, dispositions, contradictions, and findings without destructive migration.

Acceptance:

- Existing databases migrate forward and retain byte-for-byte accessible legacy artifacts; rollback can ignore new tables without losing old data.
- Observation, inference, hypothesis, open question, direct intent, inferred intent, and decision cannot be stored interchangeably.
- A claim invalidated at commit B is not returned as current at B or later, remains queryable historically, and can be superseded only with new evidence.
- Bounded exhaustive tests cover interval edges, supersession cycles, evidence reachability, and contradictory current-authority states.

**Red gate:** Attempt to create current authority without evidence, with an unknown commit, with a validity gap, or through a supersession cycle; every write must be rejected transactionally.

Risks:

- Over-normalization could make ordinary reads expensive and agent usage cumbersome.
- Backfilling legacy prose may imply claim precision the original records never had.

**Practice basis:** GP1, GP6, GP9, GP11, GP12, GP18, GP27, GP35<br>
**Baseline evidence:** `mcp-server/src/schema.sql`, `mcp-server/src/tools/evidence.ts`, `mcp-server/src/tools/contradictions.ts`, `dev/adr/0002-temporal-claim-model.md`, `mcp-server/src/tools/claims.ts`, `mcp-server/test-temporal-claims.mjs`

#### A2 — Change impact and invalidation engine

**Status:** done<br>
**Owner:** Amanuensis<br>
**Depends on:** A1<br>
**Metrics:** M2, M3, M4

**Outcome:** A commit range produces an explainable set of directly and transitively affected claims, seams, findings, and obligations without invalidating unrelated knowledge.

**Why:** Precise impact is the economic hinge: too little recall makes the record unsafe, while excessive invalidation destroys the promised time savings.

Deliverables:

- A predicted-diff artifact listing changed files, mapped objects, traversed relations, invalidated claims, and unaffected controls before comparison with expected results.
- A deterministic impact cascade using file ledger, evidence links, explicit cross-references, seam endpoints, and git rename data.
- A pluggable escalation point for lexical or model-assisted relation discovery only when explicit relations leave a measured gap.
- An impact explanation API and durable invalidation events.

Acceptance:

- The known-invalidation fixture reaches the expected claim and downstream obligation with no hidden manual expansion.
- The benign-refactor fixture stays within the false-invalidation guardrail and explains every invalidated object.
- Two-sided sensitivity checks show that adding and removing each relation class changes impact output in the predicted direction.
- The explicit-relation implementation is measured before any model or embedding fallback is admitted.

**Red gate:** Remove a required cross-reference or seam edge from the fixture; the predicted impact must lose the corresponding downstream claim and the coverage gate must turn red.

Risks:

- Static relations can miss runtime coupling and generated-code dependencies.
- Model-assisted impact can create nondeterministic invalidation noise.

#### A3 — Obligation-driven revalidation scheduler

**Status:** done<br>
**Owner:** Amanuensis<br>
**Depends on:** A2<br>
**Metrics:** M4, M11, M12

**Outcome:** Invalidated knowledge becomes a bounded, prioritized queue whose completed work lands back into the conspectus and whose omissions remain visibly red.

**Why:** Detection without custody only creates a better stale list. Full automation requires a closed loop from change to assigned work to verified landing.

Deliverables:

- A durable obligation table with trigger, destination, owner, blocking state, resolution evidence, and attempt history.
- A scheduler that scopes work packets from invalidated claims and their evidence neighborhoods, with budgets and retry policy.
- Dispatched/landed/scored reconciliation and dead-letter handling.
- Dashboard views for blocked, retried, deferred, and orphaned obligations.

Acceptance:

- Every invalidated authoritative claim creates at least one owned destination or a hard failure explaining why none can be formed.
- A dropped, duplicated, timed-out, or unscored worker result prevents run completion and remains queryable.
- Retries use unique attempt and replicate identifiers; a retry cannot overwrite earlier evidence.
- The scheduler stays within configured source, budget, concurrency, and authority boundaries during an unattended fixture run.

**Red gate:** Silently drop one dispatched obligation and duplicate another; completion must remain red with separate missing and duplicate diagnoses.

Risks:

- Queue throughput can become a proxy target that rewards shallow closures.
- Aggressive retries can amplify provider cost or repeat correlated errors.

**Practice basis:** GP20, GP23, GP24, GP25, GP29, GP33, VP4, VP11, VP18<br>

#### A4 — Verified resolution and projection read-back

**Status:** done<br>
**Owner:** Amanuensis<br>
**Depends on:** A1, A3<br>
**Metrics:** M1, M5, M12

**Outcome:** Fixed, revalidated, and materialized become evidenced states rather than optimistic labels.

**Why:** The current asymmetry—proof required to rule out but not to mark fixed—undercuts the core claim that users should not have to redo verification.

Risks:

#### A5 — Unattended refresh run envelope

**Status:** done<br>
**Owner:** Amanuensis<br>
**Depends on:** A0, A3, A4<br>
**Metrics:** M4, M11, M12

**Outcome:** A fully surveyed repository can be refreshed end to end from a commit range with deterministic controls, bounded provider use, explicit stop conditions, and a complete audit trail.

**Why:** Full automation is a systems property. It requires authority, idempotence, reconciliation, and recovery—not just a prompt that continues without asking.

Deliverables:

- A refresh command/API with plan, execute, resume, cancel, and read-back states.
- An execution manifest pinning repository SHA, source set, model/runtime configuration, budgets, determinism settings, and unique run/replicate IDs.
- Idempotency and crash-recovery contracts for every mutating stage.
- Authority-policy enforcement for configured providers, data boundaries, decision gates, code mutation, and external side effects.

Acceptance:

- An interrupted run resumes without duplicated authoritative claims or lost obligations.
- A determinism control actually changes the consulted runtime path and is visible in the run manifest.
- Configured analysis providers run without interactive approval while out-of-envelope providers or side effects stop before dispatch.
- The final status is computed from durable landed state and read-back, never from worker success messages.

**Red gate:** Crash after dispatch and before landing, then resume; if work duplicates, disappears, or completion relies on the pre-crash success signal, A5 fails.

Risks:

- Provider APIs differ in determinism, retries, and data retention.
- A permissive default envelope could turn analysis automation into unauthorized action.

**Practice basis:** GP8, GP10, GP16, GP24, GP25, GP33, VP11, VP18, VP20<br>

#### A6 — Impact-aware review brief

**Status:** done<br>
**Owner:** Amanuensis<br>
**Depends on:** A2, A4, A5<br>
**Metrics:** M5, M6, M7, M9

**Outcome:** A reviewer receives the smallest sufficient situation model for a change: diff, affected claims, constraints, history, seams, contradictions, obligations, and explicit unknowns.

**Why:** The product's value is not more context; it is the right evidence and prior work arranged around the current decision.

Acceptance:

- The brief declares uncovered files, omitted sections, and budget-driven truncation before recommendations.

**Red gate:** Supply a diff whose only known risk is in an affected seam; if the seam obligation is omitted or appears without provenance, publication halts.

Risks:

- Task-focused compression may hide a weak but important cross-cutting signal.
- Retrieval quality can be confused with underlying record quality.

#### A7 — Independent review generation and refutation

**Status:** done<br>
**Owner:** Amanuensis<br>
**Depends on:** A6<br>
**Metrics:** M5, M6, M7, M11

**Outcome:** Candidate review findings are generated and attacked by role-separated passes with controlled context differences, then aggregated without contaminating independent judgments.

**Why:** A large context and a self-critique prompt do not create independent scrutiny. Consequential findings need a refutation path designed around correlated failure.

Deliverables:

- Generator, refuter, and verifier role contracts with different evidence access and no pre-aggregation deliberation.
- A context-set diversity policy that varies evidence slices and analytical frames, not just role labels.
- Blind fixtures using surface-identical marker/control cases and treated-versus-clean diffs.
- An aggregation record retaining disagreements, defeated hypotheses, and the evidence that moved status.

Acceptance:

- A refuter can overturn a seeded false positive only by attaching new disproving evidence.
- Independent passes land before aggregation and cannot read each other's conclusions during judgment.
- Marker-only and treated-versus-clean controls remain blinded through scoring and do not produce directional contamination.
- Same-context, varied-context, and heterogeneous-runtime conditions are reported separately with test-retest stability.

**Red gate:** Reveal expected findings or another pass's verdict to a blinded reviewer; the harness must mark the run contaminated and exclude it from efficacy claims.

Risks:

- Nominal agent separation may preserve correlated context and model errors.

## Measurement contract

Metrics are reported per repository, commit range, mode, model/runtime configuration, and replicate. They are never pooled across those boundaries.

| ID | Metric | Definition | Gate | Exclusions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Survey completion integrity | Required obligations present and valid divided by all obligations required by the pinned inventory, concern, seam, run, and export manifests. | 100%; any missing blocking obligation keeps completion red. | Files and concerns explicitly excluded before execution with owner and reason remain in the denominator as satisfied exclusions; post-hoc exclusions are forbidden. |
| M2 | Stale-claim recall | Expected invalidated authoritative claims reached by the impact engine divided by all invalidated claims in the labeled fixture. | ≥95% before targeted invalidation replaces the conservative fallback; 100% for critical seeded claims. | Unlabeled claims and changes outside the pinned commit range are reported separately, not silently removed. |
| M3 | False invalidation rate | Still-valid authoritative claims withdrawn by the impact engine divided by all still-valid claims in the benign and mixed fixtures. | ≤10% initially and lower than conservative subsystem invalidation by a preregistered margin. | Claims whose validity cannot be adjudicated stay unknown and are not counted as true negatives. |
| M4 | Human revalidation reduction | One minus targeted-refresh active human verification minutes divided by full-resurvey active human verification minutes for the same repository state and quality gates; machine wall time, provider cost, and token use are reported alongside as separate units. | >30% human-time reduction with no decrease in M2 or known-defect recall before living refresh is the default; machine cost must remain within the A0-preregistered guardrail. | Setup and cache warm-up are included for both paths; failed or abandoned runs remain in reported time and cost. |
| M5 | Actionable precision | Review findings accepted as correctly evidenced and action-relevant divided by all review findings presented as actionable. | ≥80% on the evaluation corpus, reported by severity and finding class. | Unverified suspicions are a separate product output and cannot be removed from the review-volume report. |
| M6 | Missed-contract rate | Known applicable contracts absent from the review brief or integral assessment divided by all applicable labeled contracts. | 0 for critical contracts and <5% overall before super review is recommended for consequential changes. | Unsupported languages or repository regions remain explicit unsupported strata, not exclusions from the headline. |
| M7 | Human verification minutes | Elapsed active human time from furnished brief to accept, reject, or correctly defer the review or design output. | Lower median than repository-native review with non-inferior correctness; report distribution and repository strata. | Training, interruption, and tool-failure time are tagged and reported both included and separated. |
| M8 | Constraint preservation | Explicit user, code, operational, and compatibility constraints retained correctly through brief, option matrix, lean, and decision projection divided by all stated constraints. | 100% for hard constraints; soft constraints may be traded only with an explicit consequence. | Contradictory constraints stay in the denominator and require an underdetermination result. |
| M9 | Unknown retention | Known gaps and unresolved contradictions visible with a destination in the final artifact divided by those present in the source situation model. | 100% for blocking unknowns and ≥95% overall. | Resolved items require resolution evidence; compression alone is not resolution. |
| M10 | Research decision yield | Consumed research requests that change a named option, premise, confidence reason, or decision divided by all completed research requests. | Establish baseline first; rescope admission if yield stays below 20% for two evaluation cycles while backlog grows. | Explicit exploratory research is reported separately and cannot improve the decision-yield numerator. |
| M11 | Test-retest stability | Across at least two runs with identical pinned inputs: exact agreement on critical status and blocking-obligation IDs, plus Jaccard agreement on noncritical authoritative object IDs; report both components and metric step size. | No critical-status disagreement; quantitative target set after A0 baseline rather than invented in advance. | Runs with different model/runtime configuration, context set, or determinism input are separate conditions. |
| M12 | Run and projection reconciliation | Expected work and durable objects that are dispatched, landed, scored, materialized, and read back consistently divided by all expected work and objects. | 100%; any mismatch is blocking. | Canceled work remains present with an authorized cancellation state and is never erased from the denominator. |

## Program risks

| Risk | Early signal | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| The product becomes a general knowledge platform and loses its code-evidence advantage. | Roadmap work adds generic orchestration, ontology, or collaboration infrastructure before M2–M7 improve. | Require every Amanuensis feature to improve a named codebase decision metric; move general session machinery behind a Chorusmith contract. | Product |
| The living record is either unsafe or as expensive as a resurvey. | M2 misses critical invalidations, M3 invalidates most claims, or M4 stays below its gate. | Keep the conservative fallback, narrow the supported operating envelope, and stop downstream feature expansion. | Amanuensis |
| Automation hides uncertainty behind a green status. | Blocking unknowns, dropped work, projection mismatches, or provider failures disappear from final artifacts. | Make obligations, reconciliation, and read-back server-side halting gates with sabotage tests. | Amanuensis |
| Architecture assistance invents intent or entrenches accidental structure. | Inferred intent is rendered as direct intent or recommendations survive contradictory goals without qualification. | Typed epistemic kinds, underdetermination tests, three non-consensus lenses, and human acceptance authority. | Amanuensis + human |
| Research and learning loops optimize volume rather than decisions. | Backlog grows while M10 falls, or unscored sessions change active policy. | Admission criteria, decision destinations, qualification, ablation, and reversible learning states. | Amanuensis + Scholiast |
| Evaluation results are attractive but not transportable. | Claims rely on one repository, pooled metrics, shared context, or a single run. | Stratified corpora, independent conditions, unique replicates, MDEs, nulls, graded controls, and explicit operating envelopes. | Evaluation |

### Kill or rescope criteria

- **Targeted refresh fails critical stale-claim recall or does not reduce verification work after two bounded design iterations:** Stop review/design expansion; retain Amanuensis as a high-integrity survey and conservative resurvey tool while reconsidering the impact model.
- **The durable record does not lower human verification minutes at non-inferior correctness:** Remove or radically simplify the system-of-record claim; optimize the brief and evidence path before adding capabilities.
- **Review, design, and generative modes cannot preserve epistemic-kind separation in blinded scoring:** Do not ship the affected mode; keep it as an explicitly experimental lens outside authoritative output.
- **Research backlog grows for two evaluation cycles while decision yield remains below 20%:** Disable automatic dispatch, retain manual research requests, and tighten admission or remove the loop.
- **Chorusmith orchestration fails behavior, evidence, recovery, or verification-time parity:** Do not extract the workflow; keep the adapter experimental and Amanuensis independently complete.
- **A provider, model, or graph layer adds cost without surviving its ablation:** Remove it and return to the simpler explicit-relation or deterministic implementation.

Limitations:

- This roadmap was authored and adversarially re-read in one agent context; it is not an independent or blinded validation of the product thesis. A7 and A16 require real independence before efficacy claims.
- Numeric gates for recall, invalidation, and precision are initial operating hypotheses, not established effect estimates; A0 and A16 may revise them with recorded rationale.
- The roadmap validator proves structure, dependency integrity, required fields, practice-accounting coverage, evidence-path existence, and projection correspondence. It does not prove that roadmap claims are true or that the plan will work.
- External Chorusmith, Scholiast, Collatio, and distillation contracts were used as planning context but are not vendored or enforced by this repository yet.
